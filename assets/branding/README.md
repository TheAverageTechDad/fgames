# Pocket arcade identity

A pocket containing mint and yellow game tiles represents a growing library of small, approachable games. The coral play pocket is the shared identifying shape. No game-specific vehicle or nut appears in the identity.

`pocket-arcade.png` is the full-resolution master generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Production assets are rebuilt with `pnpm icons`; the master is not shipped in the offline cache. The browser favicon is a simplified, hand-authored vector companion in `static/icons/pocket-favicon.svg`. Maskable exports inset the artwork for circular and rounded-square crops.

Generation prompt:

> Create one finished square mobile app icon for a library of simple offline mini games, easy to play with one hand. A distinctive pocket arcade symbol: three chunky rounded toy tiles tucked into a small coral-red pocket, with a cream triangular play cutout on the front of the pocket. Behind it two simple mint-green and golden-yellow game tiles peek out at different slight angles; one has a single dark circular pip, the other a clean plus-shaped inset. Warm ivory full-bleed background. Strong dark warm ink outlines, subtle solid offset depth like tactile wooden toys, crisp minimal vector-like shapes. Friendly premium casual game identity, immediately legible as a tiny home-screen icon. Center all meaningful artwork within the middle 68 percent of the square for maskable icon safety. No text, no letters, no cars or bolts, no game controller, no wifi symbol, no decorative sparkles, no gradients, no glossy rendering, no mockup, no multiple options. Square 1024x1024 composition, background extends all the way to every edge, do not round outer canvas corners.

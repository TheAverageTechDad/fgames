# Sound It Out

Working title for the phonics reading game. It opens on the sound map: one stepping stone per set (s a t p i n; m d g o; c k ck e u r; h b f l ff ll ss). A stone opens once the stone before it has a finished round; each round is a star (3 shown). Tapping a stone plays a round of 8 of its words: read on the slider, except the 3rd, 6th and 8th, which are Pick cards. First card on the arcade home screen, or open `/phonics`. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-phonics-game-design.md`.

- `words.ts`: the sets in school order, the words split into sounds (`ch|i|ck`, `th:dh|i|s`), the banned list and `round()`, `choices()`. `words.test.ts` checks every spelling is taught, every audio file exists and no banned word slips in.
- `audio.ts`: decoded buffers for instant playback, each clip fading the last over ~40 ms, the word chime and Dad's praise (Catcha's recordings). Follows the arcade's mute switch.
- `ReadCard.svelte`: one word. Sound buttons (dot per sound, bar for two-letter sounds), the blending rail (drag, or ArrowRight on the handle) ending in a goal ring, the join, then the child's turn: a yellow speaker counts down 3 s (or waits for a tap, per the setting) before the word is spoken, then the card turns and the picture develops out of a blur with a shine, and Next. An idle handle nudges itself after 3.5 s.
- `PickCard.svelte`: Pick. The word is said, and the child taps it among three written look-alikes from `choices()` (the closest by sound, from its own set or earlier). A wrong tap wiggles and dims that word and says the target again; the right one lifts and glows mint, sparkles, and the card turns to the picture. The face-down card is a speaker for hearing it again.
- `SoundMap.svelte`: the stones, their stars, and the bob on the stone to play next.
- `PhonicsGame.svelte`: the shell: map or round, back (round → map → arcade), 8 progress dots, settings (sound; say the word after 3 s or only when tapped; open every stone, for children who know all the sounds), the round-end card with its star. Progress and settings are in `settings.ts`, saved as `phonics_progress` and `phonics_reveal`. Read it again (↺ beside Next) and a finished word's progress dot both remount the card fresh. The screen doesn't scroll, zoom or rubber-band, and a touch starting at the screen's edge is held so Safari's swipe-back can't leave the game mid-slide.

Audio in `static/phonics/audio`, all one Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS voice (Fola, AI Studio): `sounds/{id}.m4a` (one batched request per stone; Gemini says d and g with an "uh", so those two are cut to the first ~90 ms, and its lone /e/ was unclear, so e is the vowel cut from its own "peg") and `words/{word}.m4a` (two batched requests with a warm "you read it!" style). Batches are cut at their silences, peak-normalised and checked with Whisper. Replace any file under the same name; no code changes.

Pictures in `static/phonics/pictures/{word}.webp` (and `star.webp` for words with none): Image Playground, Illustration style, "…centred, filling the picture, on a plain pale cream background, simple children's picture book", captured at 2x, the app's rounded corners cropped off, 480 px WebP. Font: Andika Bold, Latin subset, `static/fonts`.

Verify: `pnpm check`, `npx vitest run src/lib/games/phonics`, `pnpm build`.
